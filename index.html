The Candidate

A Jurist Built
for This Moment

"An independent judiciary is not a gift from those in power — it is a right extracted, defended, and renewed by every generation of Kenyans."

Ekaterina Handa has spent over fifteen years at the intersection of corporate integrity and human justice — across East Africa, Europe, and international arbitration tribunals. She has argued before the High Court of Kenya, the East African Court of Justice, and the ICC, and spent a decade building the kind of courtroom discipline that the Court of Appeal demands.

Her campaign is not about personal ambition. It is about returning the judiciary to its constitutional purpose: to be the last line of defence for ordinary Kenyans, not a revolving door for the well-connected. She brings fifteen years of deep legal practice and zero tolerance for the institutional erosion that has weakened public trust in Kenya's courts.

LLB — University of Edinburgh LLM — Sciences Po Paris Called to the Bar — 2009 Advocate — High Court of Kenya ICC Arbitration Panel LSK Member in Good Standing
The Manifesto

Six Pillars of Judicial Reform

01

Judicial Independence

Advocate for enforceable firewalls between executive influence and judicial appointments. No sitting judge should fear career consequences for an honest ruling against the state.

02

Access to Justice

Champion a public legal aid framework that reaches informal settlements and rural counties. Justice delayed by poverty is justice denied — the courts must reflect this truth structurally.

03

Case Backlog Reduction

Deploy structured case management, mandatory pre-trial timelines, and technology-assisted scheduling to reduce the Court of Appeal backlog by 40% within three years of appointment.

04

Anti-Corruption Bench

Support the creation of a dedicated anti-corruption appellate track with specialised judicial training, asset declaration for all judicial officers, and transparent disciplinary proceedings.

05

Constitutional Fidelity

Commit to the full application of the 2010 Constitution — including devolution, bill of rights, and gender equity provisions — in every decision from the bench, without political deference.

06

Judicial Transparency

Publish reasoned judgments within 60 days of hearing, open court statistics to public scrutiny, and support live-streaming of significant public-interest appeals where security permits.

On the Ground

Initiatives Already Underway

Access to Justice

Handa Mobile Legal Clinic

A rotating legal aid clinic serving informal settlements in Mathare, Kibera, and Korogocho — providing free legal advice, court representation referrals, and know-your-rights workshops in Kiswahili and Sheng.

2,400+ residents served since 2021

Legal Education

Handa Foundation Scholarship

Annual scholarships for fifteen high-achieving students from underserved counties to study law at Kenyan universities. Scholars receive mentorship, moot court training, and guaranteed internship placements.

60 scholars funded across 4 years

Court Reform

Open Docket Initiative

Partnering with the JSC and Strathmore Law School to build a public-facing case tracking dashboard — allowing litigants, journalists, and civil society to monitor case status and judgment delivery timelines.

Pilot launching Nairobi courts Q3 2025

Gender & Justice

Women on the Bench

A structured mentorship programme pairing senior women advocates with aspiring female jurists, addressing the structural barriers that keep women underrepresented at appellate and Supreme Court levels.

35 mentorship pairs active

Devolution

County Court Capacity Building

Training programme for magistrates in Northern and Coastal counties on constitutional law, land rights, and gender-based violence proceedings — funded through LSK and international partners.

120 officers trained in 2023–24

Technology

E-Filing Advocacy

Driving adoption of Kenya's e-filing system across all Court of Appeal registries, reducing physical filing barriers for upcountry litigants and reducing administrative bottlenecks that contribute to delays.

6 registries targeted for full rollout
