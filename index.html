The Counsel

Authority Built
on Principle

"The law is not merely a profession — it is an architecture of trust, constructed brick by brick, argument by argument."

Ekaterina Handa has spent over fifteen years at the intersection of corporate integrity and human justice. Trained at one of Europe's most distinguished faculties of law and tempered by practice across three continents, she brings an uncommon breadth of perspective to every mandate she accepts.

Her approach is architecturally precise: she begins with the structure — understanding not just what the law says, but why it was built that way — and then crafts arguments of both intellectual rigour and persuasive clarity. Clients describe working with her as illuminating; opponents describe it as formidable.

Beyond the courtroom, Ekaterina mentors the next generation of legal minds, contributes to international arbitration scholarship, and advocates for access to justice across emerging economies through the Handa Foundation.

LLB — University of Edinburgh LLM — Sciences Po Paris Called to the Bar — 2009 ICC Arbitration Panel
Specialisations

Areas of Practice

Commercial Litigation

Complex multi-party disputes, injunctions, and enforcement proceedings across common law and civil law jurisdictions. A record of landmark rulings in contractual interpretation and corporate liability.

Corporate Advisory

Strategic counsel on mergers and acquisitions, governance structures, regulatory compliance, and cross-border transactions. Trusted advisor to boards and C-suite executives in high-stakes decision-making.

International Arbitration

Representing sovereign states, multinationals, and individuals before ICC, ICSID, and LCIA tribunals. Specialist in investment treaty claims and state-commercial disputes with cross-cultural sensitivity.

Regulatory Defence

Navigating investigations by financial regulators, antitrust authorities, and governmental bodies. Expert in dawn-raid management, leniency applications, and enforcement appeal proceedings.

Dispute Mediation

Accredited mediator with a philosophy that the best resolutions preserve relationships. Skilled in transformative and evaluative mediation models for commercial, family, and community disputes.

Contract Architecture

Drafting and negotiating complex commercial agreements, joint ventures, licensing arrangements, and bespoke financing structures — with the precision of an engineer and the foresight of a strategist.

Beyond the Brief

The Person
Behind the Counsel

Ekaterina believes the best lawyers are the most human ones. She draws inspiration from architecture — finding in great buildings the same qualities she seeks in great legal arguments: clarity of structure, economy of means, and the quiet confidence of something built to last.

When not in chambers, she runs early mornings along Nairobi's Karura Forest, cooks Georgian recipes inherited from her grandmother, and mentors young women from under-resourced communities through the Handa Foundation scholarship programme.

Integrity above all

Every mandate is accepted on its merits, not its fees. Work that conflicts with her principles is declined — even when it is lucrative.

Community stewardship

Through the Handa Foundation, Ekaterina funds legal education scholarships for fifteen talented students from East Africa each year.

Intellectual curiosity

A voracious reader of legal philosophy, economic history, and classical architecture — she believes the best minds draw from the widest wells.

Balanced life, sharper mind

A daily runner, occasional potter, and committed home cook — she approaches rest as a professional discipline, not an indulgence.
