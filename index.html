Founded in 1974, Ashford & Merritt is a full-service law firm serving Fortune 500 corporations, sovereign governments, private equity firms, and high-net-worth individuals. Our partners bring expertise from the highest levels of government, judiciary, and international commerce.

We do not measure success merely by verdicts won or settlements reached. We measure it by the enduring strength of our client relationships — many spanning three generations — and the trust that comes from delivering counsel of the highest order.

"The law is not a system of rules. It is a living instrument of justice — and we wield it accordingly."
